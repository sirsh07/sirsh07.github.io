Regional serum PFAS comparison

Reported central concentrations and available distribution intervals across a national reference, community cohorts, and highly exposed U.S. populations.

Download data

How to read: circles are medians; diamonds are geometric means. Vertical lines for Wilmington and Uppsala are interquartile ranges. NHANES lines extend from the geometric mean to the 95th percentile because a lower percentile was not reported in the selected clinical reference. C8 values are medians without interval information.

Important limitation: this is a comparison of published cohort summaries—not a pooled individual-level distribution. Study year, age structure, recruitment, serum versus plasma matrix, assay, and exposure context differ. The log scale is necessary because the C8 PFOA median is much higher than the other central estimates.
