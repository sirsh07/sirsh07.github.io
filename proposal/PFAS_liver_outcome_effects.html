What this means for our planned analysis

The clearest primary plots are participant-level PFAS concentration versus log-transformed ALT, AST and GGT, with a nonlinear smoother and confidence band. Estimate adjusted effects using survey-weighted or cohort-appropriate regression, report a fixed exposure contrast (such as per doubling or per IQR), and test effect modification by obesity, sex and age. Analyze the mixture alongside individual compounds. FIB-4 and diagnosed NAFLD/NASH should be secondary outcomes because prior findings are less uniform and may require adequate clinical case counts.

Minimum variables and covariatesPFAS concentrations; ALT, AST, GGT, ALP and bilirubin; age; sex; race/ethnicity; BMI or waist; alcohol; smoking; diabetes; lipids; medications; kidney function; fasting status; survey design variables where applicable. Hepatitis status is important when available.
